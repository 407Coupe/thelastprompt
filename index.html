How it works

Three acts. Three evidence drops. One verdict.

The investigation unfolds around the table over hosted live by Matt Ensor.

01

Act I , Entrée

The case opens with the first sealed evidence pack: documents, photographs, witness material and the first chance to question VERITY.

02

Act II , Main

A second physical evidence drop changes the shape of the case. New records, objects or testimony expose contradictions and open deeper lines of questioning for VERITY.

03

Act III , Dessert

The final evidence arrives. Tables test their theory against the complete case, challenge VERITY, resolve conflicts and prepare a verdict.

04

The reveal

Your table names the murderer, motive and method before the final reconstruction reveals what really happened.

Physical-first

Every act puts new evidence on the table.

At entrée, main and dessert, a new physical evidence pack is released: printed records, sealed envelopes, photographs, handwritten notes, witness material and objects that move the investigation forward.

VERITY gives the evidence a voice.

Meet VERITY

VERITY seems to know everything.

Inside the story, VERITY is presented as the most powerful AI model on earth, with access to the fictional company’s entire digital world. In reality, it is live generative AI built into the experience. Ask it anything. Follow up. Test a theory. Request the source. Make it compare testimony with documents and reason across the evidence released in each act.

VERITY behaves like generative AI really does. It can be astonishingly capable, fluent and persuasive. It can also be incomplete, overconfident, overly agreeable or wrong. The way you question it can change what you discover.

The mystery is learning how much weight to give it.
