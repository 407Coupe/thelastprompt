The premise

Tomorrow changes everything. Tonight changes first.

VERITY is a transformative artificial intelligence built on one of the world’s most powerful generative AI models. On the eve of its launch, a private dinner brings together the people closest to the project.

Before the evening is over, the company’s founder is dead. Everyone around him had a reason to fear what tomorrow might bring. Some are lying. Some are hiding things. Only one committed murder.

The AI witness

Ask VERITY anything.

You do not need to understand AI to solve the case. VERITY is not a scripted chatbot. It is a functioning generative AI with access to the fictional world of the case.

It can search suspect interviews, compare documents, identify contradictions, reconstruct events, challenge your theory and point you back to the source passages behind its answers. It may know things that are not obvious in the physical evidence.

But VERITY is not omniscient. It reasons from the information available to it. It can make assumptions. It can be overconfident. And the way you frame a question can change the answer you receive.

VERITY’s answer is not the evidence.
Its sources are.

Who can you trust?
